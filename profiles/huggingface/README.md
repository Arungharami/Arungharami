## Research and datasets

- **[DriftCVE-NLP](https://github.com/Arungharami/DriftCVE-NLP):** vulnerability classification and retrieval under temporal language drift. Explore the [versioned dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/arun-gharami/driftcve-cwe) and [research portal](https://driftcve-nlp.vercel.app). Recorded experiments use chronological splits and provenance; live inference remains pending.
- **[Drift-Robust TinyML](https://github.com/Arungharami/Drift-Robust-TinyML-Research-System):** electronic-nose sensor drift, resource-aware XAI, and host-side numerical equivalence. Physical nRF52840 latency, memory, and energy measurements remain blocked. [Research portal](https://drift-robust-tinyml-research.vercel.app).
- **[Biomedical Hybrid IR](https://github.com/Arungharami/biomedical-hybrid-ir):** NFCorpus comparisons of TF-IDF, BM25, BGE, MedCPT, rank fusion, and reranking, with result artifacts and statistical analysis. [Research portal](https://biomedical-hybrid-ir.vercel.app).
- **[DriftGuard-IoT](https://github.com/Arungharami/DriftGuard-IoT):** leakage-safe intrusion detection and drift-aware evaluation. Real-data research remains blocked; synthetic development runs are non-reportable.
- **[Lead.AI benchmark engineering](https://github.com/Arungharami/lead-ai-labs-hf-upgrade):** controlled synthetic financial-risk experiments, artifact-integrity checks, and publishing workflows. Synthetic results do not establish real-world fraud performance.
